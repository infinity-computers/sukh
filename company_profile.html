SUKHDHAM ESTATE AGENCY

Sukhdham Estate Agency is a trusted family-run real estate firm established in 1998 with more than 25 years of experience in the property market. The company is dedicated to helping clients find their dream properties through expert guidance, deep local market knowledge, and long-standing relationships with customers.

Client satisfaction and timely execution of property deals are our motto. We are the specialists in managing various property types such as residential homes, luxury apartments, commercial offices, and expansive residential plots.

Setting unmatched standards, our real estate services have been widely acclaimed in and around Gujarat. With a strong presence in Bharuch and nearby areas, Sukhdham Estate Agency has built a reputation for reliability, transparency, and professional service in every real estate transaction.

Shri Girishbhai Patel, the President of the Bharuch Real Estate Association, has more than 21 years of real estate business experience. He is highly respected and popular within the real estate group, ensuring trusted and secure transactions.

Shri Jaimin Patel, our Real Estate Specialist, brings more than 8 years of enthusiastic experience to the agency, providing dynamic, modern solutions to clients navigating the property market.
